Meet your sitter

Hi, I'm Palesa — the heart behind Little Stars Babysitting.

Ever been to a family gathering? I'm the one you'd always find carrying a kid, happily dodging the cooking and the dishes. I like to think that's where it all started.

I wasn't always a patient person, but growing up looking after my younger cousins and my baby sister taught me something. Whenever I'm with kids, I feel a sense of belonging — there's something about being around younger minds, how they think, how curious they are, how different every single one of them is, and how pure and beautiful each of their souls truly are.

Through the awkward first minutes after parents leave, through hugs and memories at the end of the day, through excited screaming that makes my head spin a little, through the quiet afternoons when the sun is too hot and little bodies get confused and cranky, through every grumpy moment and every happy one — kids never stop amazing me.

"This role combines my love for children with the chance to support hardworking parents who need someone they can trust."

I understand how important it is for parents to know their children are in caring, responsible hands. That's why I treat every child with patience, kindness, and respect — creating a safe space where they can be themselves, learn, and feel comfortable while you're away.

For me, babysitting isn't just a job — it's a responsibility I truly value. My goal is to give parents peace of mind, reliability, and to make every child feel cared for.

Thank you for trusting Little Stars Babysitting. I look forward to meeting you and your little ones!
